Same room · Same WiFi · No install

Two phones.
One game.

Fun Hub hosts 5 free two-player games that run in a mobile browser. Each game is played by two people on two separate phones in the same room, paired by scanning a QR code. No app install, no account and no internet connection beyond the local network is required.

  1. Open the gameOne phone opens a game and shows a QR code.
  2. Scan to joinThe other phone scans it. Same WiFi, no app, no account.
  3. PlayThe phones talk directly to each other. One thumb each.

The games

5 games, all free, all in the browser.

  • Air Hockey

    Drag your thumb across your own half to slide your mallet and knock the puck into the goal slot behind your opponent. Each phone shows the table from its own end, and seven goals takes the match.

    2 players3 minrealtime

  • Chess

    A full game of chess played across two phones, each showing the board from its own side. Tap a man then tap its square; castling, en passant, promotion and threefold repetition are all enforced, with five minutes each and three seconds a move. Practice alone against a computer set to easy, normal or hard.

    2 players10 minturns

  • Dawn Patrol

    Drag anywhere to bank your fighter and hold the FIRE button until the guns overheat. Parachuted crates multiply your rate of fire, damage and gun count for a few seconds, while mines and flak bursts hurt whoever is nearest; five kills takes the patrol.

    2 players3 minrealtime

  • Moonbloom

    Lead your moth with a fingertip and hold it over a closed bud until the bud opens in your colour. Both phones can work the same bud and nothing you have grown can be taken back; eight blossoms wins the night.

    2 players2 minrealtime

  • Orchard

    Swipe to steer your snake around a wrapping orchard and hold to sprint. Fruit makes you longer, bombs cost a hundred, and driving your nose into the other snake scores; five hits wins the match.

    2 players3 minrealtime

Questions

Do I need to install an app?
No. Both games and the hub run in a normal mobile browser. There is nothing to install and no account to create.
How do the two phones connect?
One phone hosts and shows a QR code; the other scans it. The phones then talk directly to each other over your local WiFi using WebRTC, so no game data passes through a server.
Do both phones need to be on the same WiFi?
Yes. The connection is made directly between the two phones on the local network, which is what keeps it fast enough to play in real time.
Can more than two people play?
No. Every game here pairs exactly two phones.
Can I play on my own?
Most games include a practice mode against a bot, which needs only one phone and no connection.
